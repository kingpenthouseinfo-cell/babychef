JAMAICAN BEEF PATTIES

YIELD:
8 patties

PASTRY INGREDIENTS:
3 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
2 teaspoons kosher salt
1 tablespoon granulated sugar
1 tablespoon Jamaican curry powder
2 tablespoons turmeric powder
1 cup unsalted butter, cold and grated
1 cup ice-cold water

BEEF FILLING INGREDIENTS:
2 tablespoons olive oil
1 lb ground beef
1 large yellow onion, finely chopped
2 scallions, thinly sliced
1 tablespoon minced garlic
1 Scotch bonnet pepper, seeded and minced
1 cup beef stock
1/2 cup plain breadcrumbs

OPTIONAL EGG WASH:
1 egg
2 tablespoons water

METHOD:

1. MAKE THE DOUGH
Add the flour, kosher salt, sugar, curry powder and turmeric to a large bowl.
Whisk until evenly combined.
Add the cold grated butter.
Use your fingers to work the butter into the flour until pea-sized pieces remain.
Add the ice-cold water and mix until a shaggy dough forms.

2. PREPARE THE FILLING INGREDIENTS
Finely chop the yellow onion.
Slice the scallions.

3. COOK THE BEEF FILLING
Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
Add the ground beef and cook until fully browned.
Add the onion and cook until soft.

4. PREPARE THE OVEN
Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C).

5. DIVIDE THE DOUGH
Divide the dough into 8 equal pieces.

6. ROLL THE DOUGH
Roll each piece into a 6-inch circle.

7. FILL THE PATTIES
Spoon the filling onto one half of each circle.

8. FOLD AND SEAL
Fold the dough over the filling and press the edges.

9. CRIMP
Crimp the edges with a fork.

10. OPTIONAL EGG WASH
Whisk the egg and water, then brush over the patties.

11. BAKE
Bake for 30 to 35 minutes until golden brown.

12. COOL AND SERVE
Let the patties cool for 10 minutes before serving.
